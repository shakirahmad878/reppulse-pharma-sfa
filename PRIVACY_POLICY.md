# Privacy Policy for SefMed Pro Pharma SFA

**Last Updated**: September 5, 2026

## 1. Introduction
SefMed Pro ("we", "our", or "the Platform") provides enterprise Sales Force Automation and Field Reporting services to pharmaceutical companies and authorized field personnel. This Privacy Policy explains how location, attendance, and commercial data are collected, processed, and safeguarded.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Periodic Background Location Data**: When an employee is clocked in on duty, SefMed Pro collects periodic location pings approximately once every 15 minutes. This location data is used exclusively to:
  1. Verify physical presence within 100 meters of doctor clinics during DCR visit logs.
  2. Automatically calculate accurate travel distance for mileage reimbursement (@ ₹7.50 / km).
  3. Provide authorized Super Administrators with real-time fleet overview and route compliance history.
- **Strict Access Control**: Location telemetry is accessible **exclusively to designated Super Administrators**. Field peers, managers without authorization, and third parties cannot view employee location trails.
- **Camera & Photos**: Used solely when the user explicitly takes an attendance selfie during Punch-In or uploads a receipt photo.
- **Device Identifiers**: Device battery state, mock location status, and operating system version for anti-spoofing and support.

## 3. Data Protection & Security
All telemetry pings and personal data are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and stored at rest using AES-256 encryption. We do not sell or monetize any user data.
